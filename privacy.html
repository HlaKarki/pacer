Pacer

Pacer privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Pacer is a speed reader made by Hla Htun. It comes as a Chrome extension, an Obsidian plugin and a web app at pacer.hla.dev. This policy covers all three.

What Pacer collects

Nothing. Pacer has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server that receives your data. It makes no network requests of its own.

What Pacer keeps on your device

  • Your reader settings, such as pace, view and text size.
  • Your place in each page or file, so you can pick up where you stopped.
  • In the web app, the text you're reading and its title.
  • In the Chrome extension, the 20 most recent documents you opened in a Pacer tab: the address, the title, the text or PDF file, and the time. A reloaded Pacer tab reads its document from here, and older documents are deleted as new ones arrive.
  • In the Chrome extension, which tab a Pacer tab came from, until the browser closes.

The Chrome extension keeps these in its own browser storage. The web app keeps them in your browser's local storage. The Obsidian plugin keeps them in its data.json file inside your vault. None of it is sent anywhere. Removing the extension or the plugin, or clearing site data for pacer.hla.dev, deletes it.

When the extension reads a page

The Chrome extension reads a page only after you press its button, press Alt+Shift+P or choose a Pacer item in the right-click menu. It reads the text of that tab, or the PDF open in it, to show it at your pace. It can read a PDF from your own disk only after you turn on Allow access to file URLs.

Hosting

The web app is a set of static files served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare handles requests to the site under its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
